# Beauty Product Sales Analysis (SQL Project)

## Overview
This project analyzes a supply chain dataset using SQL to extract insights related to product performance, shipping efficiency, and customer behavior.

The goal is to use SQL to answer real-world business questions.

## Dataset
The dataset includes:

- SKU (product ID)
- Product type
- Revenue generated
- Shipping carriers and costs
- Stock levels and lead times
- Defect rates
- Customer demographics

## Business Questions

This analysis answers the following:

- Which product types generate the most revenue?
- What are the top-selling products?
- Which shipping carriers have the highest costs?
- Which products have high defect rates?
- How do lead times affect stock levels?
- How do customer demographics impact sales?

## Key Insights

- A small number of products generate most of the revenue
- Shipping costs vary significantly between carriers
- Higher lead times are associated with higher stock levels
- Some SKUs have consistently high defect rates
